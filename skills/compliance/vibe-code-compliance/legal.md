# Legal: policies, consent, data rights, ethics

## Required pages

- Privacy policy explaining what data is collected, how it is used, and who receives it.
- Terms of service.
- Refund policy if selling goods, subscriptions, or services.
- Cookie policy with a compliant consent banner for GDPR and CCPA.

## Data and consent

- Explicit consent checkboxes on forms, never pre-checked.
- Collect only necessary data, minimize PII collection.
- Data deletion request option for GDPR right to erasure.
- Age verification and parental consent for children's data under COPPA.
- Unsubscribe link in every marketing email under CAN-SPAM.
- Map all PII flows to support data subject access requests under GDPR, CCPA, and CPRA ([source](https://consilien.com/news/navigating-ccpa-and-gdpr-compliance-essential-steps-for-us-businesses-in-2025)).
- Honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Universal Opt-Out signals under CCPA and CPRA ([source](https://smartsmssolutions.com/resources/blog/business/gdpr-ccpa-checklist-ecommerce-2025)).
- Execute Data Processing Agreements with all SaaS vendors processing EU personal data ([source](https://smartsmssolutions.com/resources/blog/business/gdpr-ccpa-checklist-ecommerce-2025)).

## Ethical and legal

- Strip dark patterns such as hidden fees, trick questions, and forced continuity.
- Strip fake reviews and unsupported claims.
- Verify commercial licenses for all fonts, images, icons, and media.
- Add business details including registered legal entity and contact information.
- Disclose AI-generated code and content in client contracts to protect original work warranties ([source](https://www.designveloper.com/blog/is-vibe-coding-legal/)).
- Maintain audit logs of AI-assisted development for IP and copyright disputes per US Copyright Office guidance ([source](https://thefriedfirm.com/law-blog/vibe-coding-legal-issues-who-owns-ai-generated-code-and-can-you-safely-use-it/)).
- Audit AI-generated code for copyleft contamination (GPL, AGPL from training data) before shipping proprietary software ([source](https://dnapartners.fr/en/ai-generated-code-and-vibe-coding-copyright-licensing-and-legal-risks/)).
